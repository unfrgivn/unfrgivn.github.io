## Overview

Polaris IQ is the client-facing delivery layer for Polaris. It brings multiple apps and workflows into one cohesive experience so clients can move from reporting to decisions.

## Why it mattered

- Shifted the platform from internal enablement to a true client product.
- Created a scalable path to expand adoption without scaling headcount linearly.
- Provided a foundation for product-led growth; delivery consistency, measurable engagement, and a clear roadmap.

## My role

- Led the strategy shift toward client-facing, revenue-driving capabilities.
- Built the operating model (squads plus component leads) that enabled parallel delivery without chaos.
- Set the quality bar for client-facing readiness; access controls, reliability, release discipline, and measurable adoption.

## Impact

- Increased platform adoption and repeat usage by making the experience simpler and easier to onboard.
- Reduced operational burden through standardized external access and onboarding patterns.
- Established a durable foundation for expanding client-facing workflows and products.
