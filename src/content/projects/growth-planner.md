## Overview

Growth Planner supports scenario planning and forecasting so teams can compare tradeoffs, align on budgets, and make better decisions faster.

## Why it mattered

- Planning cycles are slow when they depend on analysts and spreadsheets.
- Decision products need to be understandable to non-experts and credible over time.
- This created a repeatable planning workflow tied to measurable outcomes.

## My role

- Led productization; converted complex modeling outputs into an executive-usable workflow.
- Set the bar for credibility and adoption; interpretability, reliability, and operational fit.
- Enabled parallel delivery through squads plus component leads.

## Impact

- Increased planning velocity and reduced manual scenario building.
- Improved stakeholder alignment by making tradeoffs explicit.
- Established a platform foundation for expanding measurement-driven decision support.
