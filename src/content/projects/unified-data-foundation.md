## Overview

This foundation is the backbone of Polaris IQ. It standardizes data from dozens of marketing platforms and turns it into consistent dimensions, metrics, and mappings that downstream apps can trust.

## Why it mattered

- Every client-facing product depends on clean, consistent data; without a strong foundation, apps become fragile and hard to scale.
- Unified taxonomy and KPI definitions reduce rework and disagreement; teams can move faster with shared standards.
- Strong governance and reliability turn data into a platform capability, not a bespoke service.

## My role

- Set the strategy to treat data as a platform product; not a one-off pipeline.
- Built the org model to scale delivery; squads delivered features while component leads protected coherence and governance.
- Drove architectural decisions and quality standards; correctness, resiliency, and operational ownership.

## Impact

- Increased confidence in reporting and decision workflows by making data consistent across platforms.
- Reduced manual mapping and cleanup effort through standardized taxonomy and KPI definitions.
- Enabled faster expansion of client-facing capabilities by providing a durable data contract for downstream products.

## What made it hard

- Multi-platform data is messy and constantly changing; schemas drift, definitions differ, and edge cases are endless.
- Governance is as much organizational as technical; standards must be enforceable without slowing delivery.

## Architecture

- Integration ingestion and normalization
- Taxonomy mapping and rule-driven governance
- KPI definition and metric standardization
- Reliability patterns and operational ownership
