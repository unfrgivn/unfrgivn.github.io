### Problem
Developers were spending too much time learning environment and DevOps skills and the time to bring in junior engineers and have them coding was unacceptable. Lack of standards also led to technology fragmentation as well as multiple solutions for the same problems within our DevOps pipelines depending on the team.

### Goals
Build a central repository of runbooks and automations for common development tasks.

### Criteria
- Automate the setup of a new developer computer through scripted playbooks.
- Provide guardrails to enforce our coding standards and SDLC.
- Easily extensible to add new features.

### Outcome
- Onboarding described as the smoothest they've ever seen by engineers from small and large enterprises.
- Consistent tools for new machine setup, creating new applications, branching off new environments, code quality and standards, testing, deployments, and documentation.
- Change management across 20+ engineers was scriptable and easily deployed for time-sensitive and coordinated rollouts.
