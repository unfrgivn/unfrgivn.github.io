## Overview

A platform modernization program that standardized Polaris infrastructure and deployments on enterprise Kubernetes and a governed cloud foundation.

## Why it mattered

- Reduced release friction and environment drift.
- Improved reliability and security posture by making environments consistent and auditable.
- Created a paved road for teams to ship faster without increasing operational risk.

## My role

- Designed the reference architecture and drove the migration program.
- Built major parts of the foundational automation; treated infrastructure as a product.
- Set standards and guardrails that enabled squads to move faster with less coordination cost.

## Impact

- Increased delivery predictability through standardized environments and automation.
- Reduced operational drag and improved confidence in releases.
- Established a scalable foundation for continued product growth and client-facing reliability.
