## Overview

Creative Audit evaluates video creative against best practices and produces clear recommendations; it makes creative quality review faster, more consistent, and easier to operationalize.

## Why it mattered

- Reduced time-to-feedback and improved consistency across teams and accounts.
- Enabled a scalable creative QA process rather than relying on scarce experts.
- Improved executive decision-making with clearer outputs and shareable results.

## My role

- Set the technical direction and delivery approach; turned an idea into a product capability.
- Worked with senior ICs on architecture; raised the technical bar and autonomy across the org.
- Ensured production readiness; reliability, consistency, and operational fit.

## Impact

- Faster creative iteration cycles; teams evaluated more assets with fewer bottlenecks.
- More consistent guidance; fewer subjective debates and late-stage surprises.
- Built a foundation for expanding AI creative capabilities over time.
